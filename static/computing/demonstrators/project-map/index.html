Public Parks Map — New Smyrna Beach

Real public places by Alex Zelenski, individual GISP — New Smyrna Beach, FL. 45 park and beach-access points from Volusia County GIS Open Data. Nothing on this map is a client project.

Filtering updates both the map markers and the project list below.

How the 45 were chosen: these are the records in the county’s Park Locations layer with a New Smyrna Beach mailing address (ZIP 32168). That filter follows the mailing address, not the city boundary, so the map reaches beyond the city limits — Lake Ashby Park and its boat ramp, about 12 miles west as the crow flies in Samsula, are the farthest. One further county record (a private facility) is excluded. Two beach-access points, 405 and 410 Beachway Ave (records VC333 and VC334), stand about 60 m apart; they are two separate county records, not a duplicated point. The county record spells one playground “Manatee All Childern’s [sic] Playground”; it is shown here with the City’s spelling, “Manatee All Children’s Playground”.

Places close together are grouped into numbered clusters; select a cluster to zoom in and separate them, or zoom in until the markers stand alone. Legend

  • Numbered cluster — several places close together at this zoom; select it to zoom in and separate them.
  • Active listed as Active in the county record.
  • Not listed means the county record leaves the status blank.
  • Coordinates are the county record points for each place (Volusia County GIS Open Data). They locate the facility, not a surveyed boundary.
  • Categories are the county record's park types; “Not listed in county record” means the county record leaves the type blank.
