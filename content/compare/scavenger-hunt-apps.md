You want a scavenger hunt for your group. The hard part is not finding an app — it is figuring out which kind of product you are actually buying. There are three different things on the market, and they solve three different problems:

- **A builder platform.** You author the missions, the app runs them. GooseChase, Scavify, and PlayTours live here.
- **A pre-built city hunt.** You buy tickets to a ready-made route in a big city. Let's Roam lives here.
- **A hosted quest kit.** You get a finished, story-driven quest to play at your own place. That is what ZQM Labs builds.

None of these is the "best" one in the abstract. The right one depends on what you want to do — build, visit, or just play. This page compares them on the terms that actually decide it: who builds the hunt, how it charges, and who it fits.

## How the price actually works out

The headline price is rarely the whole story. Work it out for your group:

- **A family of four, one evening.** A ZQM Labs Family Night kit is a $49 flat fee for the household. A GooseChase single Experience starts at $99. A Let's Roam team ticket for up to 6 players lists at $111.09. The flat-fee kit is the cheapest way to play a finished quest once.
- **A team of 20, one event.** Let's Roam team building starts at $35 per person — $700 for 20 people. A ZQM Labs Office Team kit is $349 flat for the group. A GooseChase single Experience starts at $99 for up to 10 participants, so a group of 20 needs a larger tier or a subscription (price unpublished). Scavify does not publish prices — pricing is a custom quote (third-party estimates put a small event at roughly $430).
- **Running hunts every month.** A monthly platform makes sense here. PlayTours at $35 to $79 a month covers repeated events; GooseChase subscriptions exist for frequent organizers. A ZQM Labs kit is priced per event, so it is not the cheapest way to run a hunt every week — it is the cheapest way to run one finished quest, once, well.

## What ZQM Labs is not

Fairness cuts both ways, so here is where the Lab is not the answer:

- **If you want to author your own missions**, GooseChase or PlayTours is the right tool. ZQM Labs sells finished quests, not a builder. You cannot write your own missions into a Lab kit.
- **If you want a ready-made hunt in a big city you are visiting**, Let's Roam has 3,000+ cities and a route waiting. The Lab's quests are built for your place, not theirs.
- **If you run hunts constantly**, a monthly platform subscription will cost less per event than buying a kit each time.
- **If you need a fully managed, white-glove corporate event with a dedicated account manager**, Scavify's full-service offering is built for that (pricing by custom quote). The Lab's kits are self-run by your organizer, with a one-page guide and best-effort email support.
