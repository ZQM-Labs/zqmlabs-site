## Compare before you choose

There are three ways to run a scavenger hunt: build one yourself on a platform, book a guided city hunt, or host a finished quest at your own venue. ZQM Labs is the third kind — a complete, story-driven quest you host where you already are, playable in a phone browser with no app to install.

- [GooseChase alternative](/compare/goosechase-alternative/) — a finished quest versus building your own on GooseChase: what you do yourself, what arrives ready, and what each costs.
- [Let's Roam alternative](/compare/lets-roam-alternative/) — a quest at your own venue versus a booked Let's Roam city hunt: same team, your place, your date.
- [Scavenger hunt apps compared](/compare/scavenger-hunt-apps/) — GooseChase, Let's Roam, Scavify, PlayTours, and ZQM Labs side by side on price, format, and who does the work.
