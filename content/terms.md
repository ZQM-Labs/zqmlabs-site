## Using this site

This site is the home of ZQM Labs' location quests and scavenger hunt games. The free browser demo is free to play, in your browser, with no app to install and no account needed. Demo progress stays on your own device.

A demo being playable does not mean a paid event package is available, or that an in-person route has been approved for your area. Before announcing an event, coordinate date availability, delivery area, venue permissions, accessibility, and support arrangements with the Lab.

## Hosting kits

Hosting kits are **one-time flat fees for your event window**:

- Family Night — **$49** (one household). Use code **FAMILY25** at checkout for $24 off — play for $25.
- Community — **$199** (unlimited families).
- Office Team — **$349**. Office Event — **$499**.
- Youth Groups — waitlist; ask about current availability and pricing.
- Form-Your-Own — quoted per event.

Full descriptions are on the [hosting tiers](/tiers/) and [pricing](/pricing/) pages. If these terms and those pages ever disagree, the prices on the pricing page are the current ones — and the Lab will confirm the price for your event before you promote it.

**Before purchasing:** The published checkout links may accept payment, but they do not by themselves establish that a new event window, game entitlement, download, or email delivery is available. The Halloween 2026 access window has ended. Confirm current access and fulfillment arrangements with the Lab before paying.

**No refunds or reschedules.** Kit sales are final: no refunds, and an event window cannot be rescheduled once bought.

**Kit access expires after the event window.** A kit is for the event window agreed with the Lab — it is not an open-ended license.

**Day-of support is limited.** Remote support is available through your event window as described on the pricing page; same-day, on-site support is not included.

**Custom or bespoke kits are not included.** A kit is the published tier as described. A custom route, custom story, or custom game is a separate conversation — ask the Lab, and expect a separate quote.

## What's not included

- **Printing.** You print the signs (7 pages; card stock recommended).
- **Venue or permits.** Your space, your rules — and your responsibility to get permission for it.
- **Physical props or candy.** The organizer's choice.

## Your responsibilities as organizer

- Get permission for every place on your route — a park, a school ground, or a shared community space needs its owner's permission before you print signs.
- Check accessibility, safety, route, and weather arrangements for your players, and say plainly what the route asks of them.
- Do not advertise a route, a date, a payout, or a feature the Lab has not confirmed for your event.

## Fundraisers

A Community kit can be run as a fundraiser: **families pay $15, your community keeps $5 of every sale**. Fundraising arrangements are for qualifying events and must be agreed with the Lab in writing before you promote a fundraiser. See the [scavenger hunt fundraiser guide](/guides/fundraiser-scavenger-hunt/) for a worked example.
