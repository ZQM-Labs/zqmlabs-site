## About the Lab

<details>
<summary>Is ZQM Labs only a Halloween game?</summary>

No. ZQM Labs is the studio and home for a growing collection of community-centered games. **Wisp's Lost Lanterns** is the current featured Halloween experience. Other titles may use different themes, mechanics, audiences, and ways to play.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find the games?</summary>

Visit the [Games directory](/games/). It distinguishes the currently playable Wisp's Lost Lanterns browser demo from titles that are still in development.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are games and services available worldwide?</summary>

The browser demo can be explored online, subject to the requirements shown in the game. That does not mean every game, language, hosted event, or support service is available everywhere. Confirm event logistics and support with the Lab before planning a hosted event.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are global community features live?</summary>

Do not assume features such as global leaderboards, player discovery, a centralized community platform, or specific language support are live unless the feature is explicitly documented and verified. Community participation includes published resources and opportunities to provide feedback or participate in playtesting where offered.

</details>

## Wisp's Lost Lanterns

<details>
<summary>What is Wisp's Lost Lanterns?</summary>

It is the current featured ZQM Labs Halloween scavenger hunt. Wisp the will-o'-the-wisp has lost six glow lanterns, and players follow clues and solve puzzles to recover them. [Play the free browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1) or read the [game overview](/games/hunt-for-wisps/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does a paid kit include?</summary>

Package contents depend on the current offer and must be confirmed before booking. Ask the Lab to confirm the exact materials, access, event window, organizer responsibilities, and support included in the package you are considering.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What devices do players need?</summary>

The featured experience is browser-based. Check the demo and its on-screen instructions for current device, browser, permission, camera, QR-code, and connectivity requirements before planning an event. Do not assume every future ZQM Labs game will use the same setup.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the Dial levels?</summary>

The current Wisp's Lost Lanterns materials describe four levels: Cozy Glow, Spooky, After Dark, and Overdrive. Review the in-game descriptions and choose a level suitable for your group. These levels are specific to this experience, not a requirement for every game from the Lab.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When is the current local event?</summary>

The current in-person activation is planned for Volusia County, Florida, October 30–November 1, 2026. This is not a guarantee that a particular date or package is available. Confirm details with the Lab before advertising or selling participation.

</details>

## Hosting and safety

<details>
<summary>Can we host a game in our community?</summary>

Possibly, depending on the current package, location, venue, and support capacity. Contact the Lab with your location and proposed dates before announcing an event. Online access to the demo does not guarantee in-person delivery in your region.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How much does hosting cost?</summary>

See the [hosting tiers](/tiers/) for the currently listed Wisp's Lost Lanterns offers. Prices and package details must be confirmed with the Lab before booking. These offers do not establish pricing for other games.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who chooses the route and venue?</summary>

For an in-person event, organizers must confirm a suitable venue and route for their participants, considering accessibility, supervision, local permissions, weather, lighting, and safety. Follow the current game instructions and agree on responsibilities with the Lab before the event.

</details>

## Participate and contribute

<details>
<summary>How can I help improve the games?</summary>

Explore the public [Quest Packs](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/quest-packs), [Quest Authoring](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/quest-authoring), [Organizer Toolkit](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/organizer-toolkit), and [Playtests](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/playtests) repositories. Follow each project's instructions and report issues or feedback through its stated process.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I ask a question?</summary>

For Wisp's Lost Lanterns hosting inquiries, email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) with your group or organization, location, and proposed dates. For other games, use the contact or feedback method listed on that game's page.

</details>
