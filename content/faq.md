## About the Lab

<details>
<summary>Is ZQM Labs only a Halloween game?</summary>

No. ZQM Labs is the studio and home for a growing collection of community-centered games. **Wisp’s Lost Lanterns** was the Halloween 2026 edition. Other titles may use different themes, mechanics, audiences, and ways to play.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find the games?</summary>

Visit the [Games directory](/games/). Each title should identify its status—such as playable, demo, playtest, or in development—and link to its own details or play experience when available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are games available worldwide?</summary>

The studio's creative direction is global, and the current browser demo can be explored online. That does not mean that every game, language, hosted event, or support service is available everywhere. Check the individual game's page and coordinate event logistics with the Lab before announcing an event.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are global community features live?</summary>

Do not assume features such as global leaderboards, player discovery, multilingual support, or a centralized community platform are live unless the relevant feature is explicitly announced and verified. Community participation currently includes published resources and opportunities to provide feedback or participate in playtesting where offered.

</details>

## Wisp's Lost Lanterns

<details>
<summary>What is Wisp's Lost Lanterns?</summary>

It was ZQM Labs’ Halloween 2026 scavenger hunt. Wisp the will-o'-the-wisp has lost six glow lanterns, and teams follow clues and solve puzzles to recover them. [Try the browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does a kit include?</summary>

The published offer describes printable signs, team access, the browser-based game, and organizer guidance. The Halloween 2026 access window has ended; an existing checkout link does not confirm a new entitlement or delivery. Check the hosting tiers page and confirm availability and fulfillment with the Lab before purchasing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What devices do players need?</summary>

The current game is designed for browser-based play. Check the demo and its instructions for the current device, browser, camera, and QR-code requirements before planning an event. Do not assume every future ZQM Labs game will use the same setup.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the Dial levels?</summary>

The current Wisp's Lost Lanterns materials describe four levels: Cozy Glow, Spooky, After Dark, and Overdrive. Check the game itself and the event materials for the current descriptions and suitability guidance. These levels are specific to this experience, not a requirement for every game from the Lab.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When was the local event?</summary>

The Halloween 2026 in-person activation took place in Volusia County, Florida, October 30–November 1, 2026. Coordinate availability and support for a particular event with the Lab before announcing it.

</details>

## Hosting and safety

<details>
<summary>Can we host a game in our community?</summary>

Possibly, depending on the current package, location, support capacity, and event requirements. Coordinate with the Lab — with your location and proposed dates — before announcing or selling participation. Online access to a demo does not guarantee in-person delivery in your region.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How much does hosting cost?</summary>

The current Wisp's Lost Lanterns page lists Family Night at $49, Community at $199, and Office Team / Office Event at $349 / $499. These are current offers for this game only and may depend on availability and package details. See [hosting tiers](/tiers/) and coordinate with the Lab before announcing your event.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who chooses the route and venue?</summary>

For an in-person event, organizers must confirm a suitable venue and route for their participants, considering accessibility, supervision, local permissions, weather, and safety. Follow the current game instructions and coordinate responsibilities with the Lab before announcing the event.

</details>
