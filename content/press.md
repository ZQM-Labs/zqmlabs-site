## The one-line pitch

ZQM Labs creates story-driven games designed to bring families, neighborhoods, and teams together through shared puzzles and playful adventures.

## The longer pitch

Wisp's Lost Lanterns is a Halloween scavenger-hunt experience built around six lantern stops and four selectable scare levels. The browser demo provides an entry point to the experience. Hosted-event delivery, venue suitability, package contents, and support arrangements must be confirmed for each booking rather than inferred from the existence of a demo.

## Game description

### Wisp's Lost Lanterns

The featured Halloween experience follows Wisp, a friendly will-o'-the-wisp and the Lab's first escaped creation. Teams become Lantern Keepers, solve puzzles at six stops, and work to recover the lanterns. The game is designed around four scare levels, from family-friendly to more intense play. Specific features and content depend on the player build being used.

Other seasonal concepts should be described as in development unless their individual release status and availability have been verified.
