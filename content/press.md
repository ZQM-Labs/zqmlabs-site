## The one-line pitch

ZQM Labs builds self-guided scavenger-hunt games that turn any neighborhood,
office park, or campus into a playable quest — one hour of organizer work,
no app installs, and a scare level you choose.

## The longer pitch

A community books a kit. The organizer — an honorary "Lab Assistant" — prints
seven pages and places six QR signs. Teams play on their own phones, in their
own streets, anytime during the event window. Puzzles are built for ages 5 to
75, with three hints at every stop so nobody gets stuck. A live leaderboard
keeps offices competitive. Everything runs without an app, an account, or a
download.

## Founder bio

The founder is a Volusia County GIS professional and parent who builds each
game in-house, playtests it on his own family, and answers every email
himself. The "Dr. Z" character — a cheerful mad scientist in the Frankenstein
mold — is the in-game host and brand mascot. The doctorate is a costume. The
mischief is real.

## Game descriptions

### Wisp's Lost Lanterns

The flagship Halloween edition. Wisp, a friendly will-o'-the-wisp and the
Lab's first (escaped) creation, has hidden six glow lanterns around your
neighborhood. Teams become Lantern Keepers, solve a puzzle at each stop, and
relight the lantern. Four scare levels from family-friendly to competitive.

### The Great Pumpkin Roll

The autumn festival edition. Farmer Joe's pumpkins have escaped and rolled
into town. Teams round them up across six stops with harvest-themed puzzles.

### The Snowflake Secret

The winter edition. Find the hidden snowflake ornaments and uncover the
secret of the winter festival across six stops.

## What makes it different

- **No app, no account, no download.** One phone per team, in a browser.
- **One hour of organizer work.** Print 7 pages, place 6 signs.
- **Open by design.** The authoring format, organizer toolkit, and playtest
  results are all public on GitHub.
- **Four scare levels, one engine.** Family night and office competition run
  the same game at different voltages.
- **Multi-language.** 7 languages with automatic detection.
