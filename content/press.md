## The one-line pitch

ZQM Labs develops browser-based scavenger-hunt experiences for communities,
offices, and groups. The Wisp's Lost Lanterns browser demo is available;
organizer-hosted events require separate confirmation of availability and terms.

## The longer pitch

An organizer can discuss a potential hosted Wisp event with the Lab. The published pilot format describes printable QR signs, six lantern stops and browser-based team play. The October 30–November 1, 2026 access window has ended; event dates, printable kit availability, scoring features, and delivery must be confirmed before any new event is advertised. The free browser demo does not require installing an app or buying a kit.

## Founder bio

The founder is a Volusia County GIS professional and parent who builds each
game in-house, playtests it on his own family, and answers every email
himself. The "Dr. Z" character — a cheerful mad scientist in the Frankenstein
mold — is the in-game host and brand mascot. The doctorate is a costume. The
mischief is real.

## Game descriptions

### Wisp's Lost Lanterns

The flagship Halloween edition. Wisp, a friendly will-o'-the-wisp and the
Lab's first (escaped) creation, has hidden six glow lanterns around your
neighborhood. Teams become Lantern Keepers, solve a puzzle at each stop, and
relight the lantern. Four scare levels from family-friendly to competitive.

### The Great Pumpkin Roll

A planned autumn festival edition, currently in development. Farmer Joe's pumpkins have escaped and rolled
into town. Teams round them up across six stops with harvest-themed puzzles.

### The Snowflake Secret

A planned winter edition, currently in development. Find the hidden snowflake ornaments and uncover the
secret of the winter festival across six stops.

## What makes it different

- **No app, no account, no download.** One phone per team, in a browser.
- **Organizer-guided format.** The historical event plan described six printable stops; setup and delivery for future events require confirmation.
- **Open by design.** The authoring format, organizer toolkit, and playtest
  results are all public on GitHub.
- **Four scare levels, one engine.** Family night and office competition run
  the same game at different voltages.
- **Language availability.** Check the current playable demo for supported languages; planned translations are not confirmed releases.
