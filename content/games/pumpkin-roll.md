Farmer Joe's pumpkins have escaped the patch and rolled all over town! Your team must find all 6 pumpkins and roll them back home before the festival starts.

**The Great Pumpkin Roll** is a self-guided scavenger hunt perfect for families, school groups, and community events. Works on any phone — no app download required.

## How to Play

1. **Form a team** — one phone per team is plenty
2. **Start at Stop 1** — the Starting Gate
3. **Solve the puzzle** — count, find, or read something at each stop
4. **Use hints if stuck** — 3 hints per stop, the 3rd is a near-answer
5. **Finish at Stop 6** — the Finish Line!

## What You Need

- A phone with a camera (for QR codes) or GPS
- Comfortable walking shoes
- 30–60 minutes
- A team of 2–6 people
