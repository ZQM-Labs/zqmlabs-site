## The story

Wisp — a small, wandering will-o'-the-wisp, and Dr. Z's escaped first creation — has lost her six glow lanterns, the lights that show her the way home. Without them she flickers like a dying candle. Teams become Lantern Keepers: follow the trail across six stops, solve the puzzle at each, and relight one lantern at a time. Every lantern relit makes Wisp brighter and braver — and when all six burn, the quest ends in the Lantern Parade, where Wisp lights your way home too.

Along the trail you'll meet the residents of Z's Zoo: the Welcome Pumpkin at the front door, Oakie the Whispering Oak — Wisp's best friend — the Tag Ghosts of the Giggling Graveyard, Mrs. Spider, and Professor Hoot. Keep an eye out at the parade ground for the Seventh Shadow, waving back.

Meet the creatures first in [Z's Zoo](https://zoo.zqmlabs.com), the official bestiary of Wisp's Lost Lanterns — then come back here and start the hunt.

## Play the browser demo

[**Launch Wisp's Lost Lanterns**](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1)

Meet the creatures first in [Z's Zoo](https://zoo.zqmlabs.com), the official bestiary of Wisp's Lost Lanterns.

The demo runs in your browser and does not require a paid kit or access code. Choose a team name, start the hunt, and select a scare level. Progress is stored on the current device.

This is a software demo, not proof that a paid kit is orderable or that a physical route has been approved for an event. The published demo content and available features may differ from a field-tested, organizer-configured kit.

## What to expect

- A browser-based player; no app installation required.
- One phone per team.
- Six lantern stops and four selectable scare levels.
- A built-in demo mode that does not require the production game backend.
