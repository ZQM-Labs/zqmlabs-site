The Snowflake Secret is a winter scavenger hunt! Six snowflake ornaments are hidden around town. Your team must find them all before the Winter Festival begins.

**The Snowflake Secret** is a self-guided scavenger hunt perfect for families, school groups, and community events. Works on any phone — no app download required.

## How to Play

1. **Form a team** — one phone per team is plenty
2. **Start at Stop 1** — the Frozen Fountain
3. **Solve the puzzle** — count, find, or read something at each stop
4. **Use hints if stuck** — 3 hints per stop, the 3rd is a near-answer
5. **Finish at Stop 6** — the Snowflake Finale!

## What You Need

- A phone with a camera (for QR codes) or GPS
- Comfortable walking shoes
- 30–60 minutes
- A team of 2–6 people
