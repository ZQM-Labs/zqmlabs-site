The directory distinguishes what can be played now from planned experiences. A listing marked **Playable browser demo** is available in the browser. **Playtest only** means access is limited to an organized test. **In development** means the experience is not yet a separate playable release.

## Playable demos

### [Hunt for Wisps](/games/hunt-for-wisps/)
**Playable browser demo** · Halloween scavenger hunt
Wisp has escaped again and hidden six glow lanterns. Bring your team together to solve clues, explore, and recover them in a self-guided scavenger hunt with story, puzzles, teamwork, and four selectable scare levels.

### [Lantern Player](/games/lantern-player/)
**Playable browser demo** · Lantern-lighting engine
A standalone lantern-lighting engine with story, puzzles, and atmospheric effects. Light lanterns, solve puzzles, and explore the story.

### [Wisps Tracker](/games/wisps-tracker/)
**Playable browser demo** · Live event dashboard
A live event dashboard for Hunt for Wisps events. Track teams, scores, and progress in real time.
