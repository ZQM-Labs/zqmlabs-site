The directory distinguishes what can be played now from planned experiences. A listing marked **Playable browser demo** is available in the browser. **Tool** is a companion app, not a standalone game. **In development** means the experience is not yet a separate playable release.

## Playable demos

### [Hunt for Wisps](/games/hunt-for-wisps/)

**Playable browser demo** · Halloween scavenger hunt
Wisp has escaped again and hidden six glow lanterns. Bring your team together to solve clues, explore, and recover them in a self-guided scavenger hunt with story, puzzles, teamwork, and four selectable scare levels.

Meet the creatures first in [Z's Zoo](https://zoo.zqmlabs.com), the official bestiary of Wisp's Lost Lanterns.

### [Lantern Player](/games/lantern-player/)

**Playable browser demo** · Lantern-lighting engine
A standalone lantern-lighting engine with story, puzzles, and atmospheric effects. Light lanterns, solve puzzles, and explore the story.

## Tools

### [Wisps Tracker](/games/wisps-tracker/)

**Organizer tool · Availability by arrangement**
Wisps Tracker is a companion dashboard concept for organizers, not a standalone game. Access, deployment, and current event support must be confirmed with ZQM Labs; this listing does not imply that a public self-service dashboard is currently available.
