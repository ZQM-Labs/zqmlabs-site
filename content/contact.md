## Email the Lab

**[zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com)**

Messages are reviewed as capacity permits. Reply times and event-support availability are not guaranteed unless confirmed for an agreed event window.

**Wisp game coordination:** Participants and event organizers should use this email to arrange their game. One organizer may coordinate for an entire household, office team or community group. Please share the kit format, preferred date/time and time zone, city/venue, approximate number of teams and relevant accessibility needs. The Lab must confirm availability, event scope and delivery details in writing before the event is announced or played; payment alone is not an event reservation. Never send card details or sensitive participant data by email.

[Start a Wisp game coordination email](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com?subject=Wisp%20Game%20Coordination)

## What to include

For the fastest reply, include:

| Question | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Your community, company, or group name | So we can size the kit |
| Your dates or event window | So we can check availability |
| The tier you're eyeing | Family / Community / Office / Youth / Form-Your-Own (by arrangement) |
| Approximate number of teams | Sizing and leaderboard tuning |
| Your city or venue type | Route and sign placement advice |

## Common reasons to write

**Ask about a future kit.** Paid kit checkout is currently unavailable. For a prospective community, office, youth group, or form-your-own event, confirm scope and timing in writing before announcing anything. See
[Pricing →](/pricing/) and [Tiers →](/tiers/).

**Discuss a future fundraiser.** Prior revenue-split examples are historical, not available offers. Any future arrangement needs written terms. See [Pricing →](/pricing/).

**Join the party-rental waitlist.** Tables, chairs, grills, cornhole, and yard
games are Phase 2. Get on the list and you'll hear first.

**Press and media.** See the [Press Kit →](/press/) for company facts, founder
bio, and brand assets, then email with your outlet and deadline.

**Partnerships and multi-kit bookings.** School districts, MSPs, event
planners, and multi-community organizers — email with your scope.

**Report a problem.** If a game misbehaved, tell us. Include your team name
and the stop number if you have them.
