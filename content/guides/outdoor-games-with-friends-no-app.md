Looking for GPS hide-and-seek with friends, a scavenger hunt for a
birthday, or a simple outdoor team activity? First decide whether you
want a **shared story** or an actual real-time pursuit game. The
requirements—and risks—are very different.

## Three group-friendly formats

| Format | Host effort | How to play safely |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cooperative clue walk | Short route and puzzles | One team, six approved stops, a shared finale |
| Staggered group scavenger hunt | Route and meeting-point planning | Separate starting intervals; celebrate solves, not sprinting |
| Observation bingo | Original cards, licensed imagery or venue-safe objects | Find non-sensitive details; use a readable text alternative |

One phone per team can reduce installation friction, but an advertised
browser experience still needs device, connectivity, and accessibility
testing on its current build.

## Why a GPS chase is different

Real-time manhunt or hide-and-seek can expose player positions or
encourage unsafe crossings, trespassing and unwanted pursuit. ZQM Labs
does **not** advertise live GPS manhunt, public location broadcasting,
persistent territory capture, or an automatic online multiplayer lobby.
Those are not features of the current six-stop quest system.

For a safer event, use supervised teams, a permitted walking area,
accessible fallback activities, appropriate weather breaks, and clear
end conditions. Do not set prizes based on reaching a live location
first.
