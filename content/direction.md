ZQM Labs — Z's Quests & Mischief — creates story-driven, community-centered games that bring people together through play. We are building for a global audience; local events are activations of the experience, not the limit of the brand.

## What we are building toward

### Games people can understand and share

Make each game's purpose, audience, current availability, and way to participate clear. Keep the path from discovery to demo, hosting information, and support straightforward.

### Community-centered by design

Use playtesting and organizer feedback to improve stories, instructions, accessibility, and game flow. Design for different communities and contexts instead of assuming one venue, region, or season fits everyone.

### Global ambition, honest availability

Digital discovery can reach broadly, but in-person events, fulfilment, languages, and local support require specific validation. We will say what is available now and will not imply worldwide delivery where it has not been verified.

### Reliable play and responsible operations

Test player behavior, content packs, QR/GPS flows, mobile usability, accessibility, and offline behavior before making release claims. A demo is not the same as an orderable kit, and an orderable kit is not automatically approved for a specific field deployment.

### Open resources with clear boundaries

Share public tools and approved technical resources. Keep private answer keys, paid kit materials, customer information, participant identities, and internal research systems private.

## How we distinguish release states

- **Demo:** available to explore; not proof that a paid kit or live event can be delivered.
- **Playtest:** being evaluated; feedback may result in changes.
- **Available for checkout:** the product, price, payment, confirmation, support, and fulfilment path have been tested.
- **Field-approved:** the specific route, signage/QR placement, safety guidance, accessibility alternatives, and organizer instructions have been checked for the intended event.

We will update public status when evidence changes, not simply when a page or feature is created.

## What we will prioritize

1. Reliable player and content releases.
2. Clear game and availability information.
3. Safe, accessible, well-documented organizer experiences.
4. Tested purchase/inquiry and fulfilment journeys.
5. Community feedback, localization readiness, and repeatable production.
6. New games and regions only when the quality and support model can sustain them.

## Our relationship with ZQM Computing

ZQM Computing is our distinct geospatial engineering portfolio. It develops practical geospatial patterns and tools that may support location-based experiences. ZQM Labs focuses on games and community participation. We link to public technical resources where useful, while keeping internal research systems and non-public data private.

## Accountability

We use reviewable changes, documented release states, and tests appropriate to each claim. We do not treat a successful build as proof of production health or a published page as proof of a functioning customer journey. We will measure usability, release reliability, accessibility, organizer feedback, and fulfilment after establishing trustworthy baselines.
