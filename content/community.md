ZQM Labs aims to make games that bring people together—and to give players and organizers meaningful ways to help those games improve. Community participation should be useful, safe, and honest about what is available today.

![Neighbors and teams playing a location quest together in a park](/img/community-evergreen.webp)

## Play together

Start with the [Games directory](/games/) to find the current featured experience and any other titles that are ready to try. Each game should explain its format, requirements, and current status.

## Civic, visitor and group event ideas

Planning a downtown history walk, museum quest, neighborhood story day, supervised school activity, or staff team-building experience? ZQM Labs can **discuss feasibility** using its existing six-stop story and organizer resources. A customized route, white-label arrangement, city partnership, instant checkout, or event date is **not automatically available**.

To start a scoping conversation, [email the Lab](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) with your venue/city, audience, approximate team count, preferred dates, accessibility needs and any required approvals. Do not send private participant locations or school rosters. A host should confirm permissions, safety and event delivery before making public promises.

## Help improve a game

The public repositories contain materials for the current quest system:

- [Quest Packs](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/quest-packs): sample quest content.
- [Quest Authoring](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/quest-authoring): the format and validation guidance.
- [Organizer Toolkit](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/organizer-toolkit): setup and event resources.
- [Playtests](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/playtests): playtesting documentation and feedback process.

Before contributing, follow the instructions in the relevant repository. Contributions should be reviewed and tested before being treated as part of a release.

## Community-centered design principles

- **Accessible participation:** explain device and participation requirements, and consider different abilities and environments.
- **Local context:** make clear what organizers can adapt and what requires permission, support, or a change to the game.
- **Privacy and safety:** do not assume that public leaderboards, accounts, location tracking, or social features are available or appropriate.
- **Honest status:** distinguish released games from demos, playtests, and work in progress.
- **Feedback that matters:** document issues clearly and communicate what has or has not been incorporated.

## A global direction, with honest availability

ZQM Labs intends to make games that communities can discover and enjoy across regions. That is a product direction, not a claim that every language, local event service, community platform, or support arrangement is available worldwide today.

The Halloween 2026 in-person Wisp’s Lost Lanterns activation took place in Volusia County, Florida, October 30–November 1, 2026. Coordinate availability with the Lab before announcing a hosted event elsewhere.
