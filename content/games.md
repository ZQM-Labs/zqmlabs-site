## Featured demo and past edition

### Wisp's Lost Lanterns

Halloween scavenger hunt · Past edition (Halloween 2026)

Wisp — a small, wandering will-o'-the-wisp, and Dr. Z's escaped first creation — has lost her six glow lanterns, the lights that show her the way home. Without them she flickers like a dying candle. Teams become Lantern Keepers: follow the trail across six stops, solve the puzzle at each, and relight one lantern at a time. Every lantern relit makes Wisp brighter and braver — and when all six burn, the quest ends in the Lantern Parade, where Wisp lights your way home too.

## How the current six-stop quest format works

The current Wisp's Lost Lanterns format is a self-guided scavenger hunt. No app download, no account — just a phone with a camera or GPS.

1. **Form a team** — one phone per team
2. **Start at stop 1** — follow the map
3. **Solve the puzzle** — count, find, or read something at each stop
4. **Use hints if stuck** — three hints per stop
5. **Finish at stop 6** — celebrate

## More adventures are welcome

This directory can feature games with different stories, themes, formats, and ways to play. New titles will be added when they have a public description and a clearly identified status, such as **playable**, **demo**, **playtest**, or **in development**.

We will not list placeholder titles as released games or imply that planned features are already live. Community feedback and playtesting will help shape what comes next.

## Planning a community, history or team event?

ZQM Labs' six-stop storytelling format may inform future civic and tourism pilots, school and museum activities, or team-building experiences. These are **prospective commissioned formats, not currently released city routes or instant-order products**. For venue, audience, access requirements, dates and feasibility, [email the Lab](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com). Do not announce an event or place signs before permissions and a tested delivery plan are agreed.
