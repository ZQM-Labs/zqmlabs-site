This guide is a planning aid for **Wisp's Lost Lanterns**. Confirm the current package, deliverables, organizer responsibilities, and support arrangements with the Lab before relying on any particular kit feature. A free browser demo is not the same as a confirmed hosted event.

## Before you commit to a date

- [ ] Confirm the event date, location, package price, included materials, and support contact with the Lab.
- [ ] Obtain venue permission and check any local rules that apply to signs, public spaces, parks, or private property.
- [ ] Walk the full proposed route in advance, at the time of day the event is expected to run.
- [ ] Identify an accessible alternative for anyone who cannot use stairs, uneven ground, long routes, QR codes, or location-based tasks.
- [ ] Decide who supervises children and how participants can contact an organizer or leave the activity.
- [ ] Check weather, lighting, pedestrian and vehicle traffic, and whether the route remains safe after dark.
- [ ] Test the game on the actual devices and network conditions participants are likely to use.
- [ ] Tell participants what to bring, how long the activity may take, what permissions the game may request, and whom to contact for help.

## Route and sign placement

Place signs only where you have permission. Never require players to enter roads, waterways, construction areas, private property without permission, poorly lit hazards, or restricted areas. Do not attach signs to public infrastructure or property unless the owner or authority permits it.

Keep signs visible without blocking sidewalks, entrances, ramps, emergency equipment, or sightlines. If a route becomes unsafe because of weather, crowds, darkness, or a venue change, pause or cancel the activity rather than improvising a risky detour.

## Accessibility and participant care

- Offer a route or equivalent participation option for people who cannot complete the physical route.
- Do not make a QR scan, camera use, GPS permission, hearing, color perception, or fast movement the only way to participate unless the event has a suitable alternative.
- Explain the game's atmosphere levels before play and let participants choose a level appropriate to their comfort.
- Provide a clear way to pause, skip a stop, or leave the activity.
- For children and youth groups, establish supervision and safeguarding responsibilities with the venue and participating adults before the event.

These are organizer planning considerations, not a claim that every alternative is already built into the software. Verify the actual game's capabilities during your pre-event test.

## Test before event day

1. Open the current [free browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1) on the intended phone and browser.
2. Follow the instructions and test the full player journey, not just the first screen.
3. Test each planned sign and QR code from its intended viewing distance, if QR signs are part of your confirmed kit.
4. Check whether the route and all stops work without unsafe crossings or unauthorized access.
5. Confirm how progress is stored, what happens if a phone loses connectivity or power, and whether switching devices affects progress. Do not promise recovery or cross-device continuity until tested.
6. Record the contact method for event-day problems and confirm the support hours that actually apply to your package.

## Day-of contingency plan

| Situation | Safer response |
|---|---|
| A sign is missing or damaged | Pause that stop, use only an approved replacement, and make sure participants are not sent off-route. |
| A QR code will not scan | Follow the current in-game instructions or use a tested alternative. Do not assume every stop has a map-based fallback unless verified. |
| A phone loses power or connectivity | Help the team stop safely and consult the tested recovery instructions. Do not promise progress will transfer to another phone. |
| Weather or darkness makes the route unsafe | Pause, reroute only to a pre-checked safe alternative, or cancel. Confirm any rescheduling terms in advance. |
| A participant is distressed or wants to stop | Let them stop without pressure; help them rejoin their group or leave safely. |
| The game appears broken | Record the device/browser, the step, and the visible error; contact the support channel and follow the pre-agreed contingency plan. |

## Confirmed package details

Kit contents, access methods, print specifications, support availability, response times, and event-window extensions may vary. Obtain those details in writing for the package you are booking. Do not assume a particular number of signs, access codes, printable badges, leaderboard, remote support window, or automatic recovery behavior unless the current offer explicitly includes it.
