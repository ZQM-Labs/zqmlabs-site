## What a location quest is

A location quest is a story-driven scavenger hunt played on foot with a phone: your team follows a trail of stops, solves a puzzle at each one, and finishes together. Try the [free browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1) first — it plays the same way, in your browser, with no app to install.

## Where quests suit play in DeLand

DeLand is the county seat, with a walkable historic downtown and a college community around Stetson University. **The downtown streets and the Stetson area** are the settings we start from. These are settings to coordinate, not promised routes: the exact stops, route, and permissions are agreed with the Lab for each event, and a setting only becomes a quest once the Lab has confirmed it works for your group, your date, and your players' needs.

DeLand suits school and youth groups especially well — a school club afternoon, a Scout troop outing, a church youth group — and the same format works for visiting teams and community groups. The Youth Groups tier is waitlist; the Community tier ($199, unlimited families) is a historical group tier, not currently bookable while a Youth Groups date is being arranged.

## How booking and coordination works

The free demo is playable now. A hosted local quest is by coordination — it is not booked off a shelf.

1. **Try the demo.** Play the [free browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1) so you know how a quest feels.
2. **Contact the Lab.** Email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) with your group, your dates, the tier you are eyeing, and the area you have in mind.
3. **Agree the details.** Route and stops, venue permissions, accessibility, the event window, and support are confirmed with the Lab before you announce anything.
4. **Confirm future availability.** Paid checkout is paused. Future purchase, entitlement and delivery require confirmed fulfillment and terms.
