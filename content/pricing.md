## How much does an office scavenger hunt cost?

ZQM Labs previously listed Wisp kits at **Office Team $349** and **Office Event $499**, each a one-time fee for an agreed event window. **Paid checkout is currently unavailable.** They provide the same finished, six-stop Wisp story for your organizer to run. Confirm the appropriate tier, group format, venue and dates with the Lab before announcing an event.

[Try the free browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1) · [Review reference Wisp tiers](/tiers/) · [Ask about future availability](/contact/).

**Game coordination is required.** Before organizing or participating in a future paid kit event, one organizer should email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com?subject=Wisp%20Game%20Coordination) with the proposed date/time, location, group size and kit format. Coordinate venue permission, route safety, accessibility and delivery arrangements; the Lab must confirm the event in writing. Payment alone does not confirm a booking.

## What the kit fee includes

- A ready-made six-stop Wisp quest, played in a phone browser with one phone per team.
- Printable QR signs for the stops, team access codes and a one-page organizer guide.
- Printable finisher badges, three hints at every stop and four scare/comfort levels.
- Limited, best-effort remote email support through the agreed event window.

## Budget for the organizer's work as well

| Item | What to plan |
| --- | --- |
| Kit | The published tier fee for the agreed event window; no subscription or per-player charge |
| Printing and materials | Print the kit materials; provide any optional props, prizes or candy you choose |
| Venue and permissions | Arrange a suitable permitted space; venue charges and permits are not part of the kit fee |
| Preparation | Review the instructions, prepare permitted stops and test the phone/browser setup |
| Event arrangements | Confirm group format, accessibility, safety, weather plans and the support available |

Setup time depends on the agreed format and venue. A browser demo is a way to inspect the experience; it does not establish that a route, group size or requested event date is supported. Any custom work or additional service needs a written scope before promotion.

## Compare the format before the headline price

A builder platform lets you author missions; a city hunt sells access to an existing route; a facilitated event adds services that a self-run kit does not provide. Compare the work and support included, billing unit, participant/device limits, event duration and reuse terms. Browser participation is available from multiple providers.

Use the [scavenger hunt apps and kits comparison](/compare/scavenger-hunt-apps/) for vendor and format tradeoffs, or the [GooseChase](/compare/goosechase-alternative/) and [Let's Roam](/compare/lets-roam-alternative/) comparisons for those specific options. Confirm current prices and scope with each provider.

## How to get started

1. **Try the current story.** Play the [free Wisp browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1) and review the [organizer guidance](/guides/host-your-first-scavenger-hunt/).
2. **Check your arrangements.** Send the group type/count, proposed dates, general location, tier and any suitability or support needs to [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com?subject=Wisp%20office%20kit%20and%20event%20arrangements).
3. **Confirm the scope.** Agree the event window, venue permissions, organizer responsibilities, accessibility/weather plans and available support before announcing anything.
4. **Discuss availability, not checkout.** [Contact the Lab](/contact/) about a future kit or event. No paid checkout or automatic kit entitlement is currently available; do not announce an event until scope and delivery are explicitly confirmed.
