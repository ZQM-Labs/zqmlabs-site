## What this site collects

**Waitlist email addresses.** If you join a waitlist (for example, the Snowflake Secret waitlist), the email address you enter is stored so the Lab can hold your place for that game and let you know when it is ready. Waitlist addresses are stored in a Cloudflare database and are used only for that game — they are not sold, shared, or added to any other list.

**Emails you send us.** If you email the Lab at [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com), we keep your message so we can answer it and coordinate your event.

**Demo play state.** The free browser demo keeps your progress on your own device, in your browser's local storage. That progress never leaves your device and the Lab never sees it.

## What this site does not collect

There are **no accounts** on this site — nothing to sign up for, and no passwords stored anywhere.

The site does **not run analytics or advertising scripts**, and it does not track you across the web. The only third-party script on the site is Cloudflare's email-address decoder, which unscrambles the contact email shown on the page.

The site does **not use cookies to track or advertise to you**. The site is served through Cloudflare, which may set technical cookies to deliver pages securely — those are not advertising cookies and the Lab does not use them to identify you.

## Payments

Checkout runs through **Stripe Payment Links**. Stripe processes your payment on its own pages; **ZQM Labs never sees or stores your card number**. Stripe's own privacy policy governs the payment itself — the Lab only receives confirmation that a kit was bought, and the email address the purchase was made with, so the kit can be delivered by email.

## Your choices

- **Waitlist removal:** email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) and ask for your waitlist address to be removed. The Lab will remove it.
- **Demo progress:** clear your browser's local storage (or just close the demo — progress lives only on your device).
