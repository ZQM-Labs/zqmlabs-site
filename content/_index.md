## GIS work you can inspect

See how a deliverable is put together before discussing your project:

- **Parcel map series:** the [synthetic QGIS Atlas example](https://github.com/ZQM-Computing/qgis-resources/tree/341e3376aa89c1e210727c2876ab5776e72130a0/exhibit-production) shows parcel pages, source information, coverage gaps and a delivery manifest.
- **Workflow QA:** the [synthetic automation sample](https://github.com/ZQM-Computing/ZQM-Computing/blob/870e6138f47e71d2b23676dc0fae373c1f0e588f/docs/services/examples/gis-workflow-qa-synthetic-sample.md) shows accepted records, reason-coded rejects and reconciled counts.

These are public capability demonstrations using synthetic data, not client case studies or measured customer savings. [Explore the services and approach](/computing/).

## A story you walk through

ZQM Labs makes six-stop location quests for families, teams and communities. Play Wisp's Lost Lanterns in a phone browser: one phone per team, no app to install. Choose from four comfort levels, from Cozy Glow to Overdrive.

Start with the [free Wisp browser demo](/games/hunt-for-wisps/play/?demo=1), then explore [ZQM Labs](/labs/). For paid kits, review the existing [hosting tiers and current terms](/tiers/). The [games directory](/games/) distinguishes the playable demo from information pages and [past editions](/halloween/).

## Start with the right conversation

**GIS project:** describe the map, dataset or recurring workflow you need, your deadline and the intended reviewer. [Discuss a Computing project](/computing/inquiry/).

**Game or event:** try the demo and review the [organizer information](/organizers/). A playable demo does not establish event availability.

Both brands are based in New Smyrna Beach, Florida. GIS engagements and Labs hosting have separate scopes and terms.
