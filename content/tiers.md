Looking for a **ready-made scavenger hunt kit** rather than a scavenger hunt app builder? Wisp's Lost Lanterns is a finished, story-driven six-stop quest, played in a phone browser with one phone per team. The free demo lets you examine the format before choosing a family, neighborhood or office kit. Event setup, supported locations and availability still depend on the current kit and agreed arrangements. Flat-fee kits use the same ready-made Wisp quest for every buyer; the story is not customized per buyer. Confirm the venue, event window and organizer responsibilities before announcing an event.

## Required game coordination

**To organize or participate in a kit-based Wisp game, coordinate with [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com?subject=Wisp%20Game%20Coordination).** One designated organizer may email on behalf of the whole group. Include the kit format, requested date and time (with time zone), city or proposed venue, approximate team/participant count, and any accessibility or support needs.

ZQM Labs must confirm the event window, route/venue arrangements, delivery method and support availability **in writing before your group announces or begins the event**. A payment, when checkout becomes available, does not by itself reserve a date, approve a physical route, or guarantee onsite support. Do not email credit-card numbers or sensitive participant information.

The Great Pumpkin Roll and The Snowflake Secret remain **in development**. They are not included as separately playable editions in these offers.

## What to coordinate before announcing

Availability and delivery depend on the current game, event window, venue, organizer setup, and support capacity. Before announcing an event, coordinate with the Lab on:

- The exact kit and materials included.
- The price and any fundraising terms.
- The event window and organizer responsibilities.
- Accessibility, safety, route, and weather arrangements.
- What support is available and how to reach it.
- Whether the Lab can support the requested location.

The Halloween 2026 local activation was in Volusia County, Florida, October 30–November 1, 2026. A digital demo being accessible online does not mean that in-person event delivery is available worldwide.
