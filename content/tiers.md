## Before arranging a future event

- **Checkout paused.** New kit purchases and paid entitlement delivery are unavailable until verified and explicitly reopened.
- **Refunds for non-delivery.** Orders that cannot be fulfilled are eligible for refund; applicable law and agreed terms control other refund or reschedule requests.
- **Kit access follows the event window.** Kit access for the Halloween 2026 window expired with that window (October 30–November 1, 2026). Coordinate with the Lab on current availability before announcing an event.

## What to coordinate before announcing

Availability and delivery depend on the current game, event window, venue, organizer setup, and support capacity. Before announcing an event, coordinate with the Lab on:

- The exact kit and materials included.
- The price and any fundraising terms.
- The event window and organizer responsibilities.
- Accessibility, safety, route, and weather arrangements.
- What support is available and how to reach it.
- Whether the Lab can support the requested location.

The Halloween 2026 local activation was in Volusia County, Florida, October 30–November 1, 2026. A digital demo being accessible online does not mean that in-person event delivery is available worldwide.
