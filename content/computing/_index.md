ZQM Computing provides geospatial engineering services and develops reproducible technical tools. It is distinct from ZQM Labs, the game studio that shares this website.

## Services

### [Environmental GIS production and preliminary screening](/computing/services/environmental-screening/)

Two distinct needs: scoped mapping, spatial data preparation, and figure production for environmental or engineering teams; and early-stage desktop screening for site and parcel questions. Inputs, data rights, QA expectations, review responsibility, and deliverables are established during scoping. Preliminary screening is **not** a certified survey, engineering opinion, regulatory determination, or substitute for field verification.

### [GIS workflow automation](/computing/services/gis-automation/)

Scoping, building, testing, and documenting repeatable geospatial data processing workflows. An assessment can establish current processing steps, quality issues, and a realistic baseline before any implementation or savings claim.

### [Web GIS and geospatial APIs](/computing/services/web-gis/)

Scoping and engineering geospatial web interfaces, PostGIS integrations, and application interfaces with deployment, security, and maintainability requirements.
