These resources help environmental consultants, engineering teams and project managers **specify a reviewable GIS deliverable**. They describe practical handoff controls, not regulatory determinations or a promise of a particular result.

## Choose a production guide

- [Environmental permit map checklist](/computing/resources/environmental-permit-map-checklist/) — define an exhibit index, data-source register, review status and final PDF/GIS handoff.
- [QGIS Atlas parcel map series](/computing/resources/qgis-atlas-parcel-map-series/) — use a coverage layer, unique parcel IDs and page-by-page QA to plan a consistent figure book.
- [Desktop wetland screening vs. delineation](/computing/resources/desktop-wetland-screening-vs-delineation/) — understand what public wetland indicators can and cannot establish before field and agency review.

The examples describe **possible deliverables** and use synthetic identifiers; they are not client project outcomes. For relevant technical work, see the [synthetic parcel Atlas demonstration](https://github.com/ZQM-Computing/qgis-resources/tree/341e3376aa89c1e210727c2876ab5776e72130a0/exhibit-production).
