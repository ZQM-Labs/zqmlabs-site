## Who this is for

Property buyers, planners, environmental teams, and project managers seeking an **early-stage desktop review** of available geographic data.

## Potential deliverables

- A location and context map for the supplied project boundary.
- A source-by-source screening matrix with retrieval dates, coverage, and observed limitations.
- A concise written summary of mapped constraints and items requiring professional or field verification.

## Method

Define the location and decision question, review source availability and spatial accuracy, intersect relevant mapped layers, flag data gaps, and provide reproducible evidence where licensing permits.

**Limitations:** This is preliminary GIS screening only, not a boundary survey, environmental site assessment, engineering opinion, regulatory approval, legal determination, or guarantee that no constraints exist. Parcel polygons, open datasets, hazard layers and zoning references can be incomplete, outdated, misaligned or jurisdiction-specific.
