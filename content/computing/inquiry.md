## Discuss a geospatial project

For preliminary environmental or parcel GIS screening, workflow automation, Web GIS, PostGIS or geospatial API projects, email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com?subject=ZQM%20Computing%20project%20inquiry).

To support a useful initial assessment, include only non-sensitive information: your general project geography, intended decision or workflow, deliverables, timeline, and any public data sources already available.

Do not email credentials, confidential customer records, private access keys, health information or restricted datasets. Secure transfer arrangements, if necessary, require a separate agreement.

## Before engagement

Scope, jurisdiction, availability, fees, completion dates, source-data rights and responsibilities must be reviewed and agreed independently. This preview is **not** a booking, order confirmation, price quotation, regulatory approval, or promise of an immediate response.

Preliminary GIS screening is not a certified boundary survey, engineering opinion, environmental site assessment, or regulatory determination.
