**Environmental planning, practical GIS and reproducible data workflows.**

I'm Alex Zelenski, a Certified GIS Professional based in New Smyrna Beach, Florida. My work connects environmental science and land-development planning with spatial analysis, cartography and software. Over roughly 12 years, that has included university research, environmental fieldwork, consulting practice leadership, public-sector resilience studies and open-source geospatial development.

Through **ZQM Computing**, I help consulting and operations teams turn a defined mapping or spatial-data task into a reviewable deliverable. My background is particularly relevant when environmental context, source quality and a clear explanation of the analysis matter as much as the map itself.

ZQM Computing is the portfolio name under which I, Alex Zelenski, offer independent GIS services. It is not a registered company, LLC, or DBA; proposals, invoices, and checks are issued in my individual name, Alex Zelenski.

## What I bring to a project

- **Environmental planning and permitting support.** Environmental Resource Permit support, wetland and natural-resource mapping, field/GPS data collection, mitigation and protected-species work, agency coordination, and GIS exhibits for land-development review.
- **Spatial analysis and conservation research.** Flood exposure, coastal and watershed vulnerability, critical-asset inventories, conservation prioritization and maps that explain assumptions and uncertainty.
- **GIS production and automation.** Cartographic figures, parcel and map series, spatial-data preparation, quality checks, Python processing and documented handoff workflows.
- **Technical and consulting leadership.** Requirements, scopes, proposals, budgets, multidisciplinary coordination, QA/QC and communication with clients, technical partners and public bodies.
- **Geospatial application development.** Spatial databases, web maps, APIs and reproducible examples backed by source control, tests and operational documentation.

## Professional experience

### ZQM Computing — independent geospatial developer, October 2026–present

I build and document reusable GIS tools and spatial-data workflows using Python, QGIS/PyQGIS, GDAL, GeoPandas and PostgreSQL/PostGIS. Current public examples include a parcel Atlas built on a synthetic two-parcel fixture (DEMO-P1/DEMO-P2) and a workflow QA report computed on six real Volusia County parcels (Turnbull Bay sample). ## Tools and delivery methods

My GIS work uses ArcGIS Pro/ArcMap, ArcPy, QGIS/PyQGIS, GDAL/OGR, GPS and LiDAR data, Python, SQL and PostgreSQL/PostGIS. Modeling experience includes HAZUS-MH, SLAMM and spatial exposure analysis. Recent open-source work adds GeoPandas, DuckDB Spatial, GeoParquet and geospatial APIs.

Supporting engineering experience includes Linux, Docker, Proxmox/Ceph, Git/GitHub, automated checks and deployment documentation. Tools are selected around the client's inputs, reviewer requirements and operating environment.
