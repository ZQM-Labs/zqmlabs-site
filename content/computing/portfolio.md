## Watershed, resilience and asset analysis

### Monroe County — watershed planning and GIS modeling

**Personal credit.** Stetson University credits Alex Zelenski with GIS model development, map production and second authorship of the Monroe County Watershed Management Plan. The collaborative work addressed coastal flood exposure, sea-level rise, changing habitats and critical infrastructure.

The university describes the plan as a contribution to the county's Community Rating System improvement. The insurance outcome belonged to the wider county and project team.

[Source: Stetson University project account, April 2021](https://www2.stetson.edu/today/2021/04/3-year-effort-by-stetson-professor-results-in-35-discount-in-flood-insurance-for-residents-of-monroe-county-florida/).

**Personal metadata credit.** The village vulnerability-assessment reporting identifies Alex Zelenski of Clearview Geographic as originator and contact for the critical-asset inventory. This is a documented spatial-data contribution within a larger assessment.

## Conservation planning and environmental GIS

### Lake County — resilient green-space conservation, 2023

**Technical appendix coauthorship.** Appendix A of the county's natural-lands inventory and planning-strategies report names Jason M. Evans, Megan Brinton, Alex Zelenski and Joshua Sheldon. The work combines conservation prioritization, ecological connectivity, water resources and flood resilience. My credit is to that technical appendix; the master report has a different author listing.
